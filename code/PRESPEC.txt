PRE-SPECIFICATION OF THE DEMONSTRATION RULE
Written 2026-10-13, after the minute-of-day profile (step 2) had been looked at and BEFORE any
rule profit-and-loss was computed. Nothing below was changed after the first run of demo_rule.py.

What had been seen when this was written (so the reader can judge how "pre" it is):
  - the pooled one-minute mean-return profile for EURUSD, USDJPY, GBPUSD, AUDUSD (the 16:59 and
    17:00 bars are strongly negative, 17:05 and 18:00 strongly positive);
  - the weekly close label and the 08:30 volatility peak by year and daylight-saving regime.
  No hourly or daily rule return had been computed.

RULE (one rule, no parameters fitted)
  "Short the last New York hour." Every trading day, sell the pair at 16:00 New York time and buy
  it back at 17:00 New York time. Daily profit = minus the sum of one-minute bid-to-bid log returns
  of the bars stamped 16:00 ... 16:59 New York. No cost is charged (this is what a naive backtest on
  bid-only bars does). A day counts if the hour has at least 30 bars. Monday to Friday.

PAIRS
  Headline: EURUSD, USDJPY, GBPUSD, AUDUSD. Extension (reported separately, same code): USDCHF,
  USDCAD, NZDUSD, EURGBP, EURJPY, GBPJPY. Metals are not part of the demonstration.

SAMPLE
  All data on disk, 2009-01 to 2026-10. Also reported for 2015-2026 only and year by year.

2 x 2 DESIGN
  Clock:  (a) "documented" = the vendor's stated convention, label + 5 h = UTC, then UTC -> New York;
          (b) "true" = the measured two-era convention (fxclock.label_to_utc default).
  Mask:   (a) none;
          (b) narrow mask = returns of bars 16:55 ... 17:04 New York set to zero, with New York time
              computed on the SAME clock the rule uses (a user with the wrong clock masks the wrong minutes).
  Also reported: wide mask 16:55 ... 18:04, and the New-York-for-all-years clock used in the earlier project.

STATISTICS
  Mean daily profit in basis points, standard deviation, t = mean / (sd / sqrt(n)),
  Sharpe = mean / sd * sqrt(252). No significance stars, no selection among pairs or variants:
  all pairs and all four cells are reported.

SUPPORTING SCAN (descriptive, not a second rule)
  The same statistics for "long during New York hour h" for all 24 hours, on both clocks, masked and
  unmasked, to show where a time-of-day search would land.

WHAT WOULD COUNT AGAINST THE NOTE'S CLAIM
  - If the unmasked true-clock rule does not have a clearly higher Sharpe than the masked one.
  - If the documented-clock version is as strong as the true-clock version (the clock would not matter).
  Either outcome is reported as it comes.
